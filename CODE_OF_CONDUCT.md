# Code of Conduct

TuckBit is a welcoming, technical, open-source project. Please treat contributors and users respectfully, regardless of experience, background or tooling preferences.

## Expected conduct

Be constructive, review ideas on their evidence, ask questions in good faith, acknowledge uncertainty, and help make the project approachable to newcomers. When reviewing reported model behavior, distinguish reproducible facts from personal preferences.

## Unacceptable conduct

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, publishing someone's secrets or private conversations, abusive messages, and sustained disruptive behavior are not welcome.

## Moderation and reporting

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate comments, close threads, or restrict participation as needed to keep the community safe. For platform abuse, use [GitHub's report-abuse process](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse). For security vulnerabilities, use the repository's **private vulnerability reporting** described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md); please do not share exploit details publicly.

We may refine this document as the contributor community grows.
